# USFinanceBank — US Finance Bank Online Banking

Modern, professional American banking platform for US Finance Bank.

## Overview

- **Official display name**: US Finance Bank
- **Project and brand identifier**: USFinanceBank
- **Website title**: US Finance Bank | Online Banking
- **Contact handle**: @infobank

## Color Palette

- **Navy Blue** (`oklch(0.28 0.09 262)`) for navigation, buttons, and primary sections.
- **White** for clean background and content cards.
- **Red** (`oklch(0.52 0.19 25)`) for patriotic accents, buttons, and highlights.

## Features

- **Large Banking Hero Section**: High-resolution bank headquarters photography with "Banking Made Simpler. Your Future, Your Finance."
- **Customer Sign-In & Dashboard**: Customer authentication, show/hide password, live Checking & Savings accounts, quick fund transfers, and transaction ledger.
- **Staff Sign-In Portal**: Dedicated portal for authorized personnel with role-based dashboard and audit queue.
- **Contact & Inquiries**: Verified contact channel via handle `@infobank`.
- **Responsive & Accessible**: Seamless desktop, tablet, and mobile support.
